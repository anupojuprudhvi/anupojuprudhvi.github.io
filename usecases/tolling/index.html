Engagement · U.S. Tolling Infrastructure

A shared cloud foundation. Purpose-built tolling integrations.

Multiple U.S. tolling projects relied on independently built AWS environments to support toll collection and roadside infrastructure. Each had its own operational and compliance requirements. I designed a shared, layered landing zone across 40+ accounts to standardize networking, security, and provisioning. On that foundation, I built API integrations for service-management workflows and image handling.

Why it was needed

Every account was its own island

Before this work, each tolling project's AWS environment had been built independently — its own network design, its own security posture, its own way of provisioning a new environment. That meant no consistent audit trail across the platform, security tooling rolled out unevenly, and a new environment taking weeks of manual configuration to stand up. For teams operating tolling infrastructure, that inconsistency is itself a risk.

The brief was to design a single governance model that every account — present and future — would inherit automatically, without slowing engineering teams down or requiring them to understand the underlying plumbing. The foundation use case covers how that was built; the two integration use cases show what it then made possible.
