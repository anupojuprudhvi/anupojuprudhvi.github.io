## The story · From a chat window to agents that follow the rules

*Late 2022. A failing pipeline, a dozen browser tabs, and a new thing everyone is talking about. I type my question into ChatGPT, and an answer comes back written just for me. It's confident. It's clear. And, as I'd soon learn, it isn't always right.*

That's where this story starts. It ends four years later with AI agents that read the repository, follow written standards, run their own checks, and deliver platform code and whole applications for me to review.

The other tracks on this site teach a skill. This one tells how I learned one: what I tried, what failed, and what I'd tell you if you're at the same stage today. I didn't plan any of it. Each stage happened because the one before it hit a limit, and the way forward was always the same: give AI better context, and keep a human in charge.

In short: **curious → copy-paste loop → co-worker → writer → planner → agents → standards → builder.** Each step gave AI a little more context and a little more responsibility.
