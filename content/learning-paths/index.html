Track 03 / Zero to Production

Kubernetes from Zero to Production on Amazon EKS

A path from never having used Kubernetes to running Amazon EKS in production: the basics on a free local cluster, then the core concepts (workloads, networking, storage, access, packaging), then building an EKS platform, then the real challenges of operating it. Traffic-flow diagrams throughout, and a shuffled pop quiz to end every module. No Kubernetes experience needed.

Track 04 / Break-Fix Labs

Linux for DevOps: From First Terminal to On-Call Troubleshooting

A hands-on path for new graduates and associate engineers, from a first hour on a Linux server to diagnosing real production faults. Every module ends with a break-fix lab: you break a free lab VM on purpose, then find and fix the cause with the same tools used on call. Five parts take you from the basics, through how Linux works and a troubleshooting method, to containers built by hand, hardening, automation, a capstone incident with three random faults, and reviews of real production incidents.

Ubuntu 24.04 · Bash · systemd · LVM · nftables · tcpdump · strace · eBPF · Ansible · Docker

My AI Journey: From Copy-Paste to Agentic Workflows

The story of how I brought AI into my work as a Lead DevOps engineer and DevOps architect: a curious ChatGPT question in late 2022, the copy-paste years, then AI as a co-worker, writer, and planner, and finally agentic workflows that deliver platform code compliant by design and apps built for SOC compliance. Every chapter opens with the moment that stage began and ends with an exercise to try on your own work, plus three quick scenarios.
