How a change reaches production

Every change leaves a record of what changed, who approved it, and how to undo it.

  1. 01

    Scope it

    A work breakdown structure: work packages, owners, dependencies, and the order they land in.

    Sequenced wave roadmap
  2. 02

    Raise the change

    A change request with the risk, the rollback plan, and CAB approval before anything is scheduled.

    Regulated CI/CD delivery
  3. 03

    Prove it

    Review the plan output and rehearse outside production, so the real run holds no surprises.

    Keep Aurora DR in parity
  4. 04

    Ship it

    Roll out in stages inside the agreed window, with the rollback ready rather than improvised.

    Rolling patches on Oracle RAC
  5. 05

    Verify and review

    Check the outcome, not just that the deploy finished, then close it with a post-implementation review.

    Silent CDR ingestion failure
