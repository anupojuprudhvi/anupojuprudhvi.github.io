NexusCore AI: one system for joiners, movers & leavers.
Reliable workflows, cited policy answers, sub-5s offboarding.

A platform that brings account access, joiner-mover-leaver (JML) automation, attendance, and HR policy answers into one place, instead of a dozen disconnected tools. It runs on Temporal workflows, keeps a tamper-evident (SHA-256) audit trail, and keeps each customer's data separate. It targets the modelled ~$1,400 per employee that scattered identity tooling costs.

FastAPI · Next.js 15 · Temporal SDK · PostgreSQL 16 · Amazon EKS · ArgoCD · Terraform

$240K/yr

Modelled net TCO savings (66.7% for a 500-employee company)

Sub-5s de-provisioning · 509 passing tests · 89% modelled ticket deflection
Architectural contribution
  • Built joiner, mover, and leaver workflows on Temporal: multi-step account changes across SaaS tools that retry with backoff and undo earlier steps if a later one fails, so no half-removed accounts are left behind.
  • Built policy answers that cite their source: keyword (BM25) plus vector search that quotes the exact clause heading and handbook page, with each customer's documents kept strictly separate.
  • Added per-team access profiles: ready-made access bundles for teams such as SecOps, DevOps, and Tier-2 Support, with a checklist HR works through before anything is provisioned.
  • Set up GitOps delivery on AWS: six services in separate repos, deployed with Helm to Amazon EKS and kept in sync by ArgoCD, with secrets from External Secrets Operator (and a simpler single-EC2 Docker option).
  • Put AI-assisted development on a short leash: written working rules, 509 automated tests across 5 suites (all passing), and guardrails that block raw HTML controls.
