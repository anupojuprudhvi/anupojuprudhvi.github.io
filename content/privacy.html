Privacy

What this site collects

Short version: no cookies, no analytics, no advertising or tracking scripts. The only personal data this site handles is what you choose to type into the contact form.

Browsing

Pages load only files from this site — no third-party scripts, fonts, or trackers. If you switch between the light and dark theme, that choice is saved in your own browser (local storage, key theme) and never sent anywhere.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical request data such as IP addresses for security and operations; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement ↗.

The contact form

The “Ask or message me” panel has two modes. Search runs in your browser against a list of published case studies; nothing you type there leaves your device. Message sends the name, email address, topic, and optional note you enter to my inbox through Web3Forms ↗, an email-forwarding service. If you switch to Message after a search that found nothing, the search term is added as a note — it’s shown in the summary before you press Send.

Those details are used only to reply to you. They are not added to a mailing list, shared, or used for marketing.

Your choices

You can email me instead of using the form, and you can ask me to delete a message and your contact details at any time by writing to anupojuprudhvi@gmail.com.

External links

Links to LinkedIn, GitHub, and the uptime status page open those services, which have their own privacy policies.
